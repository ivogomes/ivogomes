TapScore

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

The short version: There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. Your matches and settings stay on your device and we never see them. The one exception is the TapScore Pro purchase: to sell it and to let you restore it on another device, the app talks directly to the App Store / Google Play — no third party sits in between.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the TapScore mobile apps (iOS and Android), the TapScore apps for Apple Watch and Wear OS, and the TapScore web app. Throughout this policy, “TapScore”, “we”, and “us” refer to the developer, Ivo Gomes.

What we collect

Nothing about you. TapScore has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or any usage analytics.

The scores, match settings, and preferences you create are stored only on your own device (in the app’s local storage). They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app removes this data.

The only information that leaves your device is what’s needed to sell and restore the TapScore Pro purchase, described under Purchases below. It identifies an app installation, not a person.

Network use

  • Scoring is fully offline. Tracking a match never needs a connection, and your scores are never sent anywhere.
  • iOS and Android apps: the app asks the App Store / Google Play when it starts, to fetch the current TapScore Pro price for your region and to check whether you already own Pro. It does the same when you open the purchase screen or buy. These are the only calls the app makes; if they fail, the app carries on working offline.
  • Apple Watch and Wear OS apps: fully standalone. They make no network calls at all and send no data off your watch.
  • Web app: when opened in a browser, the page may request the “Outfit” web font from Google Fonts. That request is handled by Google and is subject to Google’s privacy policy. The mobile apps bundle the font locally and make no such request.

Purchases

TapScore Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play — we never see or receive your card or payment details, your billing address, or your store account name.

To show the right price for your region, to confirm a purchase is genuine, and to let you restore Pro after reinstalling or on a second device with the same store account, the app talks directly to Apple’s or Google’s purchase APIs on your device — no purchase data passes through or is stored on any server of ours, and no third-party purchase service is involved at all.

We do not use anything for analytics, advertising, or profiling. The purchase itself is covered by Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies.

Whether you own Pro is also recorded on your device, so the app keeps working offline once you’ve bought it.

Device features we use

  • Bluetooth clicker (optional): if you pair a Bluetooth remote/clicker, it is paired at the operating-system level. TapScore only receives the button presses as input — it does not access your Bluetooth settings, device list, or any other data.
  • Voice announcements (optional): spoken scores use your device’s built-in text-to-speech. No audio is recorded and the microphone is never used.
  • Keep-awake: the app may keep the screen on during a match. No data is involved.

TapScore does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or files.

Children’s privacy

TapScore is suitable for all ages, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. There is nothing to sign up for, and the purchase information described above carries no name, age, email address, or location.

Third parties

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The complete list of third parties involved in TapScore is:

  • Apple and Google — they distribute the app and process payments, as described under Purchases;
  • Google Fonts — for the web app only, to serve the “Outfit” typeface.

We do not sell or share your data, and none of these receive your match data. There is no advertising network, data broker, or analytics provider involved at any point.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
