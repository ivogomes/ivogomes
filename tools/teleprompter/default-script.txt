[Stage Direction] Aochen and Hans walk on stage. Aochen looks closely at a map. Hans waves happily at the crowd.

Hans: What do you mean? Look at this crowd! They look hungry. They want to eat us!
Aochen: No, look at the screen! It says “DashHack 2026”. We’re two lobsters at a hackathon!
Hans: (Gasps) Oh no! And there are already more than 20 projects registered!
Aochen: More than 20?! What are they building? New ways to cook lobster?!
Hans: Worse. Tech projects!
Aochen: Worse?
Hans: Yes! They’re engineers. They can build anything!
Aochen: Right! Pretend we know about tech. Say some computer words!
Hans: (Pointing claws at the crowd) Uh… AI! Wi-Fi! Passwords! Please don’t boil us!
Aochen: They’re not buying it! We need someone who actually knows what happened at DashHack!
Hans: Ivo, save us! Tell them about the great things they built in 2025!

[Stage Direction] Both Aochen and Hans quickly run off the stage sideways, clicking their claws. Ivo continues with the 2025 achievements.
